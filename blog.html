ViEW2026 Program AnnouncedViEW2026のプログラムが公開されました

The program for ViEW2026 (December 3–4, Pacifico Yokohama) has been published. My presentation is IS2-16, "Path generation for passing through doorways with a gaze-controlled powered wheelchair without a map," in Interactive Session 2. It is a poster presentation on December 3, 14:40–15:50. I will now prepare the poster.ViEW2026（12月3日・4日、パシフィコ横浜）のプログラムが公開され、私の発表は インタラクティブセッション2の IS2-16「地図を用いない視線入力式電動車いすの出入り口通過の経路生成」に決まりました。 発表は12月3日 14:40–15:50 のポスター形式です。ポスターの作製を進めます。

Paper Accepted to SII 2027SII 2027に論文が採択されました

Our paper on a gaze-controlled powered wheelchair, "Development of a Dwell-Based Gaze-Controlled Powered Wheelchair with Obstacle Detection and Its Evaluation at a Special Needs School," has been accepted to the IEEE/SICE International Symposium on System Integration (SII 2027) as a Contributed Paper. I will give an oral presentation at the Kobe International Conference Center in January 2027. I thank everyone at the special needs school who cooperated with the evaluation.視線入力式電動車いすに関する論文 "Development of a Dwell-Based Gaze-Controlled Powered Wheelchair with Obstacle Detection and Its Evaluation at a Special Needs School" が、 IEEE/SICE International Symposium on System Integration (SII 2027) に Contributed Paper として採択されました。 2027年1月に神戸国際会議場で口頭発表を行います。特別支援学校での評価にご協力いただいた皆様に感謝いたします。
